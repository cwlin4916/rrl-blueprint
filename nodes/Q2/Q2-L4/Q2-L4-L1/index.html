operatorsevery one of them ends by regenerating the prompts and this site

  • /decompose <question | node-id>add a question and its lemma statements, or expand one lemma into sub-lemmas. Statements only, never proofs
  • /prove <node-id>prover → render gate → critic, at most two rounds. Writes the node's proof.md, its critique and its status
  • /examples <node-id>what the literature has on one node: first whether the statement has appeared in print, then instances, simplest first
  • /check-goal <node-id>test the statement against the bench of settled cases. Cheapest check in the pipeline, and it can only lower a number — run it before /prove
  • /update [node-id]revise the tree against its own record. Never a no-op: it changes a statement, or it changes what the node is waiting on
  • /generalize <node-id>a verified claim strictly more general than the node becomes its own abstraction node, with the target left standing as the special case
  • /tighten <node-id>cut a write-up to what only it can say. The claim and every step headline are frozen, so nothing can be lost in the cutting
  • /research-statuswhere the DAG stands, and what to do next
